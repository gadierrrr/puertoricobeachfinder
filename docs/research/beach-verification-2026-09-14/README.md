Checked online: **2026-09-14**. Scope: recommendations #1 and #2, covering 20 priority page records.

## Read this first

The cohort comes from the historical Search Console targets in `migrations/035-seo-content-top20.php`. Current Search Console credentials/exports were unavailable locally. These are **not claimed to be today’s 20 busiest beaches**, and no visitor counts or rankings have been invented. The August SEO report provides historical aggregate context, not per-beach rankings. Re-rank with a current 28-day page export when available; aggregate English/Spanish URLs and resolved aliases.

This is a completed desk audit and research register, not a field-verification certificate. Source-listed means a source supports the statement, not that a facility is operating today. Undated prices and schedules remain qualified. Unknown means not confirmed, not absent. No messages or calls were made.

The public-site reader and search index supplied page content; direct requests returned 403 and some reader requests timed out. Indexed page ages ranged from today to several weeks. Findings describe retrieved versions; changing weather widgets were not used as evidence. English content was reviewed; complete Spanish parity checking is outside this audit.

## Highest-priority findings

- **Identity/location:** Ojo de Agua mixes spring and beach content; Coco Beach lists Río Mar at zero distance; Las Picúas and Punta Bandera need route/location checks; Isla Verde records need sector disambiguation.
- **Internal contradictions:** Playa de Vega parking prices; Cerro Gordo lifeguards; Black Eagle fishing advice.
- **Evidence conflict:** Caña Gorda facility/staffing claims disagree with official tourism; free Carolina parking conflicts with a historical municipal tariff.
- **Access warning:** Muelle de Azúcar’s easy-access copy needs review against a reported Ports Authority warning.
